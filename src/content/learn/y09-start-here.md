## What's inside

1. **[Tools and files](/learn/y09-tools-and-files/)**\
   Which how-to guide to use, your project folder, drawing a wireframe, naming files, and picture file types.
2. **[HTML](/learn/y09-html/)**\
   HTML is the content of your page: headings, paragraphs, lists and images. It goes in `index.html`.
3. **[CSS](/learn/y09-css/)**\
   CSS is the look of your page: colours, fonts, spacing and image sizes. It goes in `style.css`.
4. **[Fixing and testing](/learn/y09-fixing-and-testing/)**\
   What to check when something goes wrong, and how to do each test in your template.

Every topic ends with a quick **Check yourself** question. Pick an answer to see if you're right, and why.

## The order of the job

1. **Plan:** [Draw your wireframe in Draw.io](/learn/y09-tools-and-files/#draw-your-wireframe-in-drawio)
2. **Set up:** [Your project folder](/learn/y09-tools-and-files/#your-project-folder) (and the guide [Open a project folder in VS Code](/guides/open-a-project-folder-in-vs-code/))
3. **Build:** [The page skeleton](/learn/y09-html/#the-page-skeleton) (and the guide [Start a web page](/guides/start-a-web-page/))
4. **Style:** [Link style.css to your page](/learn/y09-css/#link-stylecss-to-your-page)
5. **Preview:** Go Live (the guide [Start a web page](/guides/start-a-web-page/))
6. **Test:** [Something's wrong? Check these](/learn/y09-fixing-and-testing/#somethings-wrong-check-these)
7. **Hand in:** Zip your folder (the guide [Save a version as a zip](/guides/save-a-version-as-a-zip/))

```quiz
You've built the page skeleton in `index.html`. What comes next?
- Hand in your zip
+ Link `style.css` to your page
- Draw your wireframe
- Test your page
> The order is plan, set up, build, then style. Linking `style.css` comes straight after the skeleton.
```
